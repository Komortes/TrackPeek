# TrackPeek 1.1

Build 4 · 2026-10-19 · Universal for Apple silicon and Intel.

More ways to keep playback nearby, with synchronized lyrics, keyboard controls, and reliable recovery.

## New in build 4

- English and Ukrainian translations across Settings, onboarding, player menus, help, accessibility, diagnostics and permission explanations. Russian remains available.
- Native macOS language selection, English fallback, localized decimal values and volume percentages. Choose TrackPeek’s language in System Settings → General → Language & Region, then quit and reopen the app.
- Long onboarding headings and descriptions wrap so translated text remains readable.

Build 4 includes all features and fixes from build 3 below. Existing preferences are retained.

## Highlights

- Ten desktop widget layouts, including Edge, Lyric Strip, Lyrics, Karaoke, and artwork cards. Choose a named monitor and keep that selection across reconnections.
- A compact Focus notch layout and a full-song Lyrics popover. Read ahead, return to the current line, or click a synchronized line to seek.
- Configurable global shortcuts for playback and overlay visibility. Local progress controls seek by five seconds; volume arrows adjust by one percent.
- Automatic, Normal, and Economical resource modes. Economical follows your choice without replacing saved appearance settings.
- Recovery after player restarts and temporary failures, with connection and permission diagnostics. Playback scripts run in an isolated helper so a stalled request can recover.
- Quick source selection from the player menu, improved lyrics matching, retry actions, and timing adjustments.
- A compact Settings window with even margins and a simpler order: music source, screen panel, visibility, then launch at login. Widget categories are visible immediately, placement starts with the monitor, and diagnostic check times expand on demand.

## Fixes and polish

- Automatic source selection preserves a paused song when the other player has no selected track, and avoids duplicate reads.
- Old lyrics requests are cancelled on song changes; artwork loading and shared caches reduce flicker and repeated work.
- Launch at login reflects macOS registration, explains approval requirements, and shows failures instead of silently accepting them.
- Keyboard and VoiceOver controls, labeled artwork buttons, full metadata tooltips, and clearer playback states.
- Hidden progress and lyrics clocks stop in the collapsed notch. Invalid timing and saved numeric values no longer reach unsafe conversions or geometry.
- Widget appearance shows controls that apply to the current template. Templates with no lyrics do not load text from a saved switch; MiniBar and Equalizer follow the wave-color setting independently of progress color.

## Requirements and installation

The archive supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later. The live equalizer requires macOS 14.2 or later and audio-capture permission.

Download [TrackPeek 1.1 (build 4)](https://github.com/Komortes/TrackPeek/releases/download/v1.1/TrackPeek-1.1-build4.zip). Quit a running TrackPeek before replacing it. Unzip `TrackPeek-1.1-build4.zip`, move `TrackPeek.app` to `/Applications`, and open it. Allow Automation access for Spotify or Apple Music when prompted. Build 4 is newer than builds 1–3 in Sparkle's update ordering, even though the display version remains 1.1.

This build uses an **Apple Development certificate and is not notarized**. macOS may require confirming the first launch through Open or Privacy & Security. No separate Spotify or Apple Music web login is needed.
