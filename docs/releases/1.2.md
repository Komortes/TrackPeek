# TrackPeek 1.2

**Release candidate · build 6 · not published.**

Clearer settings, steadier notch transitions, and fixes for playback and saved preferences.

## Settings

- Keep page headings, navigation, and notch/widget status visible while scrolling. The existing Enable action stays with the surface heading.
- Resize the Settings window. Wide controls move below long labels when needed, and content stays readable on larger windows.
- Compare widget templates using a larger preview rendered from the player views. Browsing a category does not change the selected template or enable the widget.
- Compare Full Player and Focus side by side, and preview menu-bar indicators, title width, and spacing beside their controls. Player previews use local sample data and do not control your music.
- Settings sliders have accessible names and formatted values, with preview text available in English, Ukrainian, and Russian.

## Notch transitions

- Text, artwork, and controls keep their layout as Standard and Focus expand or collapse. Outgoing text disappears before the next state appears.
- Interrupted transitions continue from the visible state; stale animation completions cannot restore an older state.
- Explicitly closing the panel keeps it closed under a stationary pointer. Reduce Motion and immediate layout changes stop an active resize.

## Reliability and privacy

- Seeking while paused no longer jumps back when the player briefly reports its old position. Later external seeks and track/source changes still take effect.
- Apple Music artwork recovers after temporary loading errors, and owned temporary artwork files are removed when replaced, when the client ends, and on normal app termination.
- Widget preference migration runs once and preserves values already chosen for the widget. Reset Settings remains effective after restarting instead of restoring an old panel selection.
- Lyrics HTTP responses no longer use a persistent disk cache. The bounded in-memory lyrics cache remains available.

## Requirements and installation

The planned archive supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later. The live equalizer requires macOS 14.2 or later and audio-capture permission.

Intended download **after publication**: [TrackPeek 1.2 (build 6)](https://github.com/Komortes/TrackPeek/releases/download/v1.2/TrackPeek-1.2-build6.zip). This artifact is not published yet. Once available, quit TrackPeek, unzip the archive, and replace the app in Applications. Build 6 is newer than the previously distributed builds in Sparkle's update ordering.

The planned distribution retains **Apple Development signing and is not notarized**. It is not Developer ID distribution. macOS may require confirmation on first launch.

Previous 1.1 archives and source tags remain available for manual recovery. Updating preserves existing preferences unless you choose Reset Settings.
